🛡️ Family Shield — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Developer: Zeshan Haider, Gujranwala, Pakistan

Family Shield is a parental-control app. A parent or guardian uses it to block YouTube, games, the Play Store and other chosen websites on family members' phones, to set rest time and bedtime, and to turn a phone's internet off. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

1. Data we collect

  • Parent account: the parent's Google account name and email address, used only to sign in and to identify the house owner.
  • Family member phones: a name entered by the parent or member (for example "Ali"), the phone's make and model, Android version, last-online time, current block status, and the time of any "ask to unblock" request.
  • House settings: the house name, join codes, block schedules, extra blocked websites or app names entered by the parent, and subscription status.

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect, store or send browsing history, website addresses visited, messages, photos, contacts, location or app content.
  • The website filter works entirely on the phone. Web traffic is never routed to our servers.

3. Use of Android VpnService

Family Shield uses Android's VpnService only to filter and block websites on the device, as set by the parent or guardian, and to turn the internet off during rest time. The VPN is local. It does not send traffic to a remote server, and it does not collect, sell or share browsing data. Domain lookups that are not blocked are passed to a public DNS resolver (Google Public DNS, 8.8.8.8) exactly as the phone would normally do. The app asks for clear consent before turning the VPN on.

4. How data is stored and shared

The data in section 1 is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database), hosted by Google in Singapore. It is shared only between the phones that belong to the same family house. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Payments in the Google Play version are processed by Google Play. We never see card details.

5. Children

Family Shield is intended to be set up and controlled by parents and guardians. A child's phone is joined to the house by the parent. The only data stored for that phone is what is listed in section 1.

6. Retention and deletion

A parent can remove any phone from the house in the app, and that phone's data is deleted. To delete a whole house and its data, email us from the parent's Google account and we will delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app on a member phone stops all data collection from that phone.

7. Security

All data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Database security rules allow only the parents of a house to read that house's data.
