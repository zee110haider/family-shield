🛡️ Family Shield: Kids Control — Delete your account and data

Developer: Zeshan Haider

Remove one family phone (in the app)

  1. Open Family Shield on the parent phone.
  2. Find the phone under Family phones.
  3. Tap Remove, then Yes. That phone's data is deleted immediately.

Delete your whole account and house

  1. Email haider110zeeshan@gmail.com from the Google account you use as the parent, with the subject "Family Shield delete my account".
  2. Include your 6-digit house code (shown on the parent screen).
  3. We delete your account and house within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your sign-in account, house name and codes, family phone names and models, online status, block settings, schedules, unblock requests and subscription status are all permanently deleted. Nothing is kept, except payment records that Google Play keeps under its own policies.
